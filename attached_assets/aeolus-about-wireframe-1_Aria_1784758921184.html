About Aeolus

Six decades of tire manufacturing.

Aeolus has been dedicated to tire manufacturing since 1965. Founded as Henan Tire Factory, Aeolus has grown into a global tire manufacturer with strong expertise in OTR and TBR tire segments.

Over the years, Aeolus has expanded its international presence, developed advanced tire technologies, and built partnerships with major equipment manufacturers around the world. In the early 2000s, Aeolus entered a new stage of growth, working with local distributors to grow our presence in more than 150 countries, and was listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange.

Today, Aeolus continues to focus on tire innovation, manufacturing quality, and sustainable development, supporting global customers through reliable products, advanced engineering, and strong supply capabilities.

Our History

A long history of tire manufacturing innovation.

Aeolus has a long history of tire manufacturing innovation, technical development, and global growth. Key milestones include:

Founded
1965

The Henan Tire Factory is founded, beginning six decades of continuous tire manufacturing.

First in China
1987

Produces China's first bias giant OTR tire, the 36.00-51.

Tubeless Giant
1998

Produces China's first bias giant OTR tubeless tire, the 36.00-51.

Public Listing
2003

Renamed Aeolus Tyre Co., Ltd. and listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange.

Radial OTR
2007

Aeolus radial OTR tires launch to the market.

Capacity
2011

Annual radial OTR production capacity reaches 240,000 pieces.

OEM Partnership
2012

Cooperation begins with SANY.

OEM Supply
2013

Agreement signed with Volvo, supplying tires to its factory in Sweden.

Technology License
2016

Technology license contract signed with Pirelli for the industrial segment. The 46/90R57 enters the market.

OEM Partnership
2021

Cooperation begins with Develon.

Capacity
2023

Annual radial OTR production capacity reaches 320,000 pieces.
